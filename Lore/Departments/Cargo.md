# Cargo/Shipping/Logistics - Lightspeed Long-Haul
Part of the main reason for the colony's existence is the presence of the planet along a burgeoning shipping route. Due to the slow, if steady, growth, most major shipping conglomerates avoided this sector to focus on higher-earnings potential. This left the perfect shipping-container sized hole for Lightspeed Long-Haul Shipping to step in.

## Background

Lightspeed Long-Haul came into being as a small long-haul shipping company a few decades ago. Operating out of a backwater section of the galaxy gave them space to grow away from major giants, and as such they prospered in their small region of space. The colony was one of the first major permanent outposts that Lightspeed has had the chance to start, and as such, operates as a small money sink and profit generator for the company. If it does well, they're going to cement themselves on a developing trade route. Since the colony is only one of their ventures, however, they're not likely to collapse if the colony outpost goes under. As such, they don't tend to watch the colony like a hawk.

This doesn't mean that everything is profit profit profit, however. Lightspeed was founded on the ideals of good service. The company frequently authorizes reasonable emergency spending for their freighter pilots, even if it would be a bit of a loss, as they've managed to keep ahold of the idea that long term profit through repeat customers is more important than infinite growth (at least in their small sector where word of mouth matters.) Additionally, some of the more corporate-minded higher-ups have began trying to push this through brand recognition. Periodically, employees will receive "mandates" from upper management to try and "spice up" the store, including promotions, sales, and other strange tactics like trying out slogans. These are rarely mandatory, especially since the main company rarerly makes its way out to the colony to check if they're being followed. 

## The Colony Itself

Lightspeed didn't found the colony. Hell, they weren't even the first to bid on a buisiness slot. However, they provide an important service to the colony as the major shipping and receiving terminal, as well as providing small services like vendor restocking, janitorial, and hydroponics. (Hydroponics was chosen because the corporate bean-counters found it a lot cheaper to provide seeds and have the colony grow their own food than to ship it in from offworld.) 

Lightspeed also brought in one of their upcoming subsidiaries, Lightspeed Mining Solutions, to run the colony's mineral extraction. The double whammy of a suitable quarry location nearby and a major shipping route provided them with the perfect excuse to build up a smaller portion of their company. As a result, the colony's mining is done through Lightspeed, though the [previous companies](/Lore/Departments/Prospectors) had mining bids on the planet and may even have started setting up on other areas before Lightspeed arrived. 

## Organization

Lightspeed is run by a Surface Operations Manager, or Manager, who coordinates mining, cargo, and service. Miners mine the raw materials and process them for distribution to the colony. Cargo technicians handle mail, shipping and receiving, and goods requests. Service handles the bar and kitchen, hydroponics, and janitorial. Lightspeed really does it all, at least as long as you don't need exceptionally skilled nonmanual labor. 

## Piracy and Contraband
Due to the fact that the colony is located in a backwater sector there's plenty of piracy. Illegal and legal goods, grey areas, and smugglers abound. Because of this, some of Lightspeed's workers have taken to making dealings with pirates on the side. None of these are officially recognized, but Lightspeed's freighters are seldom impacted by piracy in this region, so rumors abound that the founder herself made a deal with some of the major pirate bands in the region. It is rare to see Lightspeed employees directly dealing with pirates in broad daylight, but under the table deals are unofficially known to happen, if it'll bring in needed profit.
