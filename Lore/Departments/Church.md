# The Chorus of the Burning Circle

> "In girum imus nocte ecce et consumimur igni." - Inscription found above the door of many Choral buildings.

Formed a few hundred years ago, the Chorus of the Burning Circle represents five things: Unity, Change, Death, Rebirth, Freedom. Each of these is inscribed in a pictogram contained within one section of the church's symbol: A circle divided into five pie-slices, like a wheel.

## Worship and Beliefs

Chief among the beliefs of the Chorus is that divinity resides within every sapient being. What makes this different from most other religions is that the Chorus believes this to be literal. Every being has a missing portion of their psyche, and when all are joined in the Great Song, all sapient life will be able to ascent to their rightful status as the divine. However, to do this, life must change itself such that it is susceptible to this internal divine influence. 

There are five subparts to this belief: Unity, Change, Death, Rebirth, and Freedom
1. **Unity**: The Chorus, paradoxically, values both Unity and Freedom. Unity of purpose and vision is important to the Chorus. Each member must be knowledgeable of and working towards the end goal of remaking every sapient in the image of the Burning Circle, connecting all in the great chorus. They are not mindless drones, simply ensuring that there is a unified will presented in their actions. Because they require unity, forced conversion to the Chorus is unheard of, as that would cause significant discord in the local group.
2. **Change**: The Chorus also embodies change. Change in belief, change in body, change in life, all aspects of change are represented. Even joining the Chorus, the Induction, is symbolic of an individual leaving their old self behind and being reborn as something new.
3. **Death**: Not macabre, indeed quite the opposite. The Chorus believes death is part of a cycle, with all things being reborn infinitely until the great chorus completes and the universe can be united as one. The surgical procedure to prepare the body for the cruciform represents the death of the old member's self. Before this point, they are in the dark regarding the glory of the Song, knowing nothing about the higher purpose of life in the universe.
4. **Rebirth**: Not born-again, but recycled. Members of the Chorus believe that one's personality is remade through the ritual of the cruciform, with the Burning Circle touching their heart and mind, connecting another voice to the Song anew. After their rebirth, members are blessed with the knowledge required of the Great Song and the local plan, though their minds are still their own and they are free to pursue the plan as they like or are directed by their superiors while working.
5. **Freedom**: The Chorus, paradoxically, values both Freedom and Unity. Once reborn, members are adhered to the overarching unified will of the Chorus as a whole. However, within smaller groups, they are generally offered great leeway in how they go about their business so long as it is not inharmonious with other groups. 

Each individual member of the Chorus has different values they place the most importance on, which has lead to some friction with other factions, especially the [Port Authority](/Lore/Departments/Internal-Security), as the Port Authority has been attempting (and failing) to retrieve warrants for Chorus property to investigate reports of a death cult due to an unfortunate interaction with a group of members who valued the Death and Rebirth cycle more than the Port Authority found reasonable.

Additionally, the emphasis on the natural cycle of death and rebirth has put the Chorus slightly at odds with [Research](/Lore/Departments/Science), as the Chorus believes that simplifying the natural beauty and dvinity inherent in the cycle is part of what is preventing sapient life from exercising its full potential as masters of reality.

## Organization
> **OOC NOTE**: Tenders are not an actual role in the Chorus in-game. It is simply a term used by the Chorus to refer to people who are members who are doing another job at the moment or who work with but are not part of the Chorus. Additionally, while there can only be one Composer-Imago per round due to limitations on Heads of Staff, this position being multifaceted means that many people can play the role, just not at the same time.
{.is-info}

On the colony, the Chorus is organized as follows:
**Tender** - Anyone associated with the church. They usually don't have a cruciform and instead assist the church for their own reasons. 
**Chorister** - The basic member. They have an implanted cruciform and are privy to internal church secrets. They also are given access to the Song, a power that the Chorus works to make miracles. 
**Melodist** - A specialized member of the church, usually providing knowledge that a select few choristers know or need to know, such as construction, crafting, or history.
**Composer-Imago** - The local leaders of the Chorus. A multifaceted position, the local Composer-Imagines (Ih-mah-jin-ehs, not ih-mah-jihns) work together to lead the local Chorus. 

Outside the colony, the Composer-Imagines meet somewhat infrequently to discuss direction and the Great Song, partaking in various rituals, meetings, and discussions to determine the best path towards ascension. 

## The Cruciform

Even if the Chorus has tense relations with scientists, they are not above developing technology. Chief among the technology developed by the Chorus is the Cruciform, a small, perfectly symmetrical, cross shape formed of an unknown metal. The Cruciform is a few inches tall and wide, and is embedded in the skin of the chest when an individual is inducted into the Chorus. Whatever this object is, it is the conduit by which members of the Chorus work their miracles into reality. While in possession of a Cruciform, members of the Chorus are able of effectively casting magic, paracausal phenomena that defy most laws of science. 

The above information is what is known to the general public. To Choristers and above, it is known that the reasons for the dimensions of the Cruciform are very specific. They match, exactly, the shape of what Divinity is believed to be. The Burning Circle is the cycle of life, death, and reimagination of the self that pushes sapient life towards divine perfection. It is not the shape of their god. It is also rumored that there is an actual hole in the sapient psyche, a puzzle piece cut out of the collective unconscious, that matches this shape.

Known to the Composer-Imagines is that this rumor is true. There is, in fact, a hole in the noosphere in this exact shape, and that by filing down and rebuilding the self enough times, they believe that sapient life can fit this hole, and therefore, become divine. 

## The Chorus on the Colony
On the colony the chorus provides various religious services, printing of technology, and the processing of biological waste into biomatter. Biomatter is a mandatory portion of most Choral technology, representing the rebirth of nonsapient life into another form, and sometimes being used by other departments for various reasons.
