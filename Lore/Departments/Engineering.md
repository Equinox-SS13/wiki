# Flux Habitation Solutions
Engineering!

## Background

Flux Habitation started as a [Vulpikanin](placeholder) startup originally focused on habitation technology like temperature and climate control, lighting, and similar for spacecraft. They spread out significantly to find newer markets, eventually finding this local sector.

However, upon trying to break into the local engineering space, they found little success. A lack of funding and conections made actually obtaining contracts extremely hard, and the company was on the verge of collapse when The Benefactor stepped in.

Flux Habitation was contracted by the local government to construct the colony buildings and maintenance. They don't own the colony, far from it, but they do have a rather intimate knowledge of (and a hand in) the way that most of the buildings worked. With the crash of the Northern Light and the recent incursions by random wildlife, they've also taken up fence and gate repair, as well as some light deconstruction work in the ruins outlying the colony. 

Off-colony is a small R&D lab that produces new designs, the actual corporate headquarters building (located on the nearby planet of [PLANET](placeholder)), and The Benefactor.  

## The Benefactor

Nobody knows who, or what, The Benefactor is. All that is known is that they are fabulously wealthy, fabulously connected, and fabulously *weird*. Orders from The Benefactor come down in very strange ways. Some of the stranger ones include being literally written on a physical floppy disk, carved on fruit, and once by *actual carrier pigeon.* 

It is theorized that The Benefactor provided the original startup capital for Flux Habitation in order to place them on the colony itself, as shortly after the construction was finished the Northern Light crashed into the quarry. It is also rumored that The Benefactor had a hand in selecting [Extera](Lore/Colony) as the site for the colony as they knew that the crash was going to happen. This is not confirmed, nor is it denied, as The Benefactor refuses all forms of communication that do not request clarification on orders or are acknowledgements thereof.

## Structure

Flux Habitation is structured like a traditional craft guild, with prospective employees apprenticing at the company for a few years before being offered full employment. Traditionally, this apprenticeship is paid fairly well, and the treatment is generally good enough for people to actively seek a standard of pay below average. 

Above the Apprentice is the Engineer, the basic workhorse of Flux Habitation. Engineers are expected to teach Apprentices, handle construction and repair in the colony, and generally perform upkeep. They may also be asked to produce some items by [Lightspeed Long-Haul](/Lore/Departments/Cargo), as some of Flux's new tech has made it to market and local traders might be interested in it.

Above the Engineer is the Chief. The Chief is expected to know everything (within reason) there is to know about the colony's repair and construction. They might not know the ins and outs of every little bit, but they're expected to be able to do anything an Engineer can do, plus the stress of being a member of command staff. One of the most important responsibilities of the Chief is to be the receiver for The Benefactor's requests and communicate them to the rest of Flux's engineering team on the colony.
