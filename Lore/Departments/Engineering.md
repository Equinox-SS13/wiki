# Flux Habitation Solutions
Engineering!

## Background

Flux Habitation started as a [Vulpikanin](placeholder) startup originally focused on habitation technology like temperature and climate control, lighting, and similar for spacecraft. They spread out significantly to find newer markets, eventually finding this local sector. 

However, upon trying to break into the local engineering space, they found little success. A lack of funding and conections made actually obtaining contracts extremely hard, and the company was on the verge of collapse when The Benefactor stepped in.

## The Benefactor

Nobody knows who, or what, The Benefactor is. All that is known is that they are fabulously wealthy, fabulously connected, and fabulously *weird*. Orders from The Benefactor come down in very strange manners, through parchment, floppy disk, scrawled on fruit, and once by *actual carrier pigeon.* It is theorized that The Benefactor provided the original startup capital for Flux Habitation in order to place them on the colony itself, as shortly after the construction was finished the Northern Light crashed into the quarry. It is also rumored that The Benefactor had a hand in selecting [Extera](Lore/Colony) as the site for the colony as they knew that the crash was going to happen. This is not confirmed, nor is it denied, as The Benefactor refuses all forms of communication that do not request clarification on orders or are acknowledgements thereof.
