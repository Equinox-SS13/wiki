# Salix-Alba Relief Society

The Salix-Alba Relief Society is a burgeoning interplanetary diaster response non-profit founded privately on the planet Aberatsa. It provides free services to anyone who asks in almost every form conceivable; from pop-up field trauma centers, to ground, air, and space amulances, as well as routing primary care in underserved areas, they create a wide foundation that serves almost all medical needs. Reliant purely on donations and governmental grants, nobody would call SARS "cutting edge", but they do very well with what they have at hand. SARS Staff are more than just healthcare providers, as they often have to broker deals locally for more medical supplies, a venture which has resulted in accusations of contributions to illegal drug manufacturing to maintain their own stocks. Alongside that, SARS paramedics are armed as a matter of policy, both for self defense in unstable areas and the defense of their patients, another decision that has stirred controversy. 

## Background

The organization was made by a grup of first responders after the 2637 Harribitixa Earthquake, which necessitated the urgent need for a capable and rapid responsive disaster relief organization. SARS cut its teeth four months after its founding, rapidly mobilizign a field hospital in response to the Altzaiura Spaceport Bombing. This competent and rapid response with only a fledgeling staff gained widespread recognition of the organization, resulting in explosive growth of both staffing and governmental funding. Seventeen years later, it is now the primary disaster relief organization in the aberatsan system, and has sent out expeditionary teams to provide aid in nearby star systems as well. 

Despite being kept afloat by a constant stream of government funding and private donations, the society is sorely underfunded. Supplies and medications are often used well past their expiration date, and the allegations of shady dealings are all but fact. Similar allegations of strongarming by paramedics have surfaced as well, cilminating in plenty of valid reasons to be wary of the organizaiton. Despite this, few can accuse them of providing substandard care with what they have on hand. A tentative partnership with [Research department](/Lore/Departments/Science) has helped alleviate some of their woes, but inadequate logistics still show through.

The primary reason that SARS has found its place in the colony is simply because there's little money to be made in the healthcare sector here. Any private healthcare symposium would hemorrhage money in such a backwater setting, whereas SARS is at home in a place with plenty of resources and partners to help cultivate a self-sufficient resource chain in a low-volume environment. 

## In the Current Day

With the establishment of [the colony](/Lore/Colony), SARS was contracted to provide a small full-time aid station. With a relatively small compliment of a single doctor, three nurses, and three paramedics, their inital setup was little more thna a couple of large tents. Initially, they were set up to handle workplace accidents and general healthcare, but with the crash of the Northern Light, SARS activated their emergency response system in full force. A Task force of doctors, nurses, paramedics, and construction technicians arrived in only 36 hours. The initial aid station was expanded to a major field hospital, which soon became a properly-constructed medical bay only three weeks after the crash. 

Now, the {COLONY NAME} Salix-Alba Infirmary is a permanent medical station specifically outfitted as a critical-access emergency department. With very few specialists on hand, spare supplies, and high-acuity injuries, they are often stretched thin in both terms of staffing and equipment. Reduced from the initial response, the hospital is left with only a handful of staff augumented by doctors and former employees of the Northern Light. Many SARS staff are on time-based contracts, resulting in a combination of a complement of rotating staff and some full-time, permanently-stationed staff. 

## Who Works for SARS?

SARS members are paid employees, not volunteers nor contractors. While volunteer positions exist, they're all non-clinical support roles such as receptionists and cargo loaders. Three divisions exist within SARS: Medical, Engineering, and Logistics. The Medical division is home to trauma surgeons, critical care paramedics, nurse anesthetists, and many more medical professionals. The Engineering division has architexts, structural engineers, and a vast team of skilled tradesmen. Supporting all of them is a robust Logistics division with a network of interplanetary ferries and ground-based logistics. Of these, the Medical division is the most represented in the colony through the hospital itself, while a few members of the Engineering and Logistics divisions have taken up contracts with [Engineering](/Lore/Departments/Engineering) and [Cargo](/Lore/Factions/Cargo) respectively.

There is a tradeoff for having such a capable network in such a poorly-funded organization: many in SARS are making noncompetitive wages. Most medical staff take a pay cut to work for SARS, made up for with the unique experiences and skillsets that SARS brings forth. In spite of that, staffing is a never-ending issue, and there are a handful of low-profile whistleblowers reporting lax hiring standards. SARS, in reality, is heavily supported by people who can't find employment anywhere else. It's a refuge for physicians escaping malpractice accusations, or paramedics who can't or won't be hired anywhere else. Oftentimes, paramedics are refused in hiring beause they legally can't possess a firearm. Many nurses who have had their licenses stripped or had to resign in disgrace find a home in SARS. This isn't to say that SARS is full of criminals or malpracticioners, however, they just are supported by anyong they can find. 

# SARS Departments

SARS consists of five departments, three of which are directly represented in the colony. 

## Colony-Represented Departments

The following departments are represented on the colony directly through the Medical department. 

### Rapid Response Detachment

Members of the Rapid Response Detachment (RRD) are healthcare and engineering staff who maintain day jobs in Aberatsa but are ready to mobilize at a moment's notice. The RRD is SARS' pride and joy, and their primary service in significant incidents. SARS boasts of having a 16-hour window from receiving notice of an off-world disaster to having personnell and suplies en-route to the location, and all thanks to the RRD. 

There are three rapid response detachments on Aberatsa, one in each city. Each has a full contingent of ER and ICU staff, five EMS teams, two surgical teams, a chemistry section, and an engineering section. Their primary responsibility is incident management, triage, and field hospital construction. Many of the structures are prefabricated and simply require menial installation and stabilization, allowing for extremely rapid creation of a casualty collection area. Some members of the RRD work for SARS full-time like any member of the clinical department, but remain ready for deployment. Members of the RRD are distinguished by black scrubs with cyan shoulders, with dual horizontal stripes denoting their role.

### EMS Department

The EMS Detachment is one of the sustaining survices of SARS. Any citizen of Aberatsa has seen a SARS ambulance, rotorcraft, or shuttle.They provide EMS in most rural areas and also perform interfacility transpotation and coverage support when the primary EMS system gets too busy. Additionally, they are the primary provider of interplanetary patient transport. While SARS never charges patients for care, they do provide very strong funding incentives to local governmetns by tying ambulance coverage or staffing ability with payment, leading to EMS services becing one of SARS' primary income sources.

The EMS Department has a curious quirk and nonstop point of contention inside and outside of SARS: all paramedics and EMTs are required to be armed with a firearm while on-duty. This can occasionally create issues in emergency response to high-security areas, not to mention the yearly news stories describing SARS paramedics using a firearm in self-defense. Despite this, it has been a policy from the beginning of the organization and SARS leadership has no intention of letting it go anytime soon. Being found without your firearm while on duty (without good reason) is cause for reprimand, and repeated instances can lead to termination.

Policies do allow paramedics to disarm themselves in certain situations, however their firearm must be placed in a safe location or safe put in every SARS vehicle. Generally, disarmament is at the discretion of the most-trained provider on the crew, but is only to be done to de-escalate or minimize risks of harm on specific incidents. For example, a psychiatric patient who is known to be unarmed but physically combative, where having a firearm is a serious risk, is such an incident. Ground paramedics wear the standard Class A/B uniform or a polo shirt. The majority of the uniform is a light grey with cyan shoulders and white stripes for visibility.

### Clinical Department 

The clinical department is healthcare as most people know it. Most healthcare members of SARS fall under the clinical department, providing routine inpatient and outpatient services wherever a SARS facility can be found. These are the people who staff the permanent hospitals in far-off colonies and also provide permanent support after the RRD finishes their work. 

The clinical department consists of every echelon of healthcare staff: medical technicians, nurses, general practice and specialized physicians, respiratory therapists, surgeons, and ever-beloathed clinical management. They staff field hospitals, long-term hospitals, standalone EDs, clinics, and everything in-between. Members of the clinical department can be distinguished by their role-colored scrub tops and cyan scrub bottoms.

## Nonrepresented Departments
The following departments are not present phsyically within the colony, having served to assist in the construction and management of the hospital and initial disaster response.

### Engineering Department

The Engineering Department is what makes SARS *work*. The fancy equipment and field hospitals can't function if they don't have a team of professionals who know how to build and install them. The enginering department profides these professionals, and is almost as large as the healthcare departments are in staffing, and are equally as busy. 

Engineers for SARS will often remain on-site with field hospitals and long-term hospitals, but in downtime are authorized and encouraged to seek work with local engineering firms and comapnies. SARS engineers are considered some of the best in the industry due to their practice and experience in rapid fabrication and installation of complex and sensitive equipment. 

### Logistics Department

The Logistics Department is the supply chain of SARS. With such a large system, they society can't rely on outside shipping companies. Requiring such a system to support disaster relief, they created their own. They do provide commercial shipping services at significant cost, which makes for another source of income for the organization. Their ned for rapid, high-volume resupply makes SARS Logistics a solution for rapid shipping needs for large quantities of goods. 

Despite their importance, the Logistics Department is the neglected child of SARS. Money is diverted towards healthcare to buy new and exciting toys while freighter pilots and crew work multi-day shifts to support nonstop requisition requests. Accidents are not uncommon and cargo is often damaged in transit, all of which compounds to worsen the already-ubiquitous supply problems that SARS establishments face.
