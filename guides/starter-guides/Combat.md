## Unarmed Combat

### Martial Arts
Grab-fu is the act of tossing someone around until they die, unlike regular unarmed combat where you beat the shit out of someone. There are many things you can do with a grab, but first you have to grab them. Once you've managed to grab, either press Z or click them again with the grab in your active hand. This upgrades the grab from a <span style = "color:yellow"> passive (yellow) </span> to <span style = "color:teal">aggressive (blue)</span>. do this again to upgrade from an <span style = "color:teal">aggressive (blue)</span> grab to a <span style = "color:red">neck (red)</span> grab.

Once your grab is aggressive at least, you can do some special things here!
| Attack   | Intent | Minimum Grab Level | Method | Result |
|:----------:|:-------------:|:-------------:|:------:|:-------------:|
| Dropkick | <span style = "color:red"> Harm </span> |  <span style = "color:teal"> Aggressive </span> | Target their chest. | Deals either 30 or ROB/3 damage, whichever is higher, and knocks the target away from you. |

| Fireman's Throw | Any | <span style = "color:yellow"> Passive </span> | Click on yourself while grabbing someone. | Throws the person you've grabbed behind you forcefully.
| Force Vomit | <span style = "color:green"> Help </span> | <span style = "color:teal"> Aggressive </span> | Grab their mouth. | Forces the target to vomit.|
| Gut Punch | <span style = "color:red"> Harm </span> |  <span style = "color:teal"> Aggressive </span> | Target their groin. | Punches the target for 1 or 10 minus the target's TGH/4, whichever is higher. Then, makes them vomit. |
| Headbutt | <span style = "color:red"> Harm </span> |  <span style = "color:teal"> Aggressive </span> | Target their head. | Deals 20 damage + 3 times your headgear's damage. Additionally, deals you 10 damage and makes the target dizzy. If they're not wearing head protection, can paralyze them and knock them unconscious. |

## Safety and Firemode

Before you can fire a weapon, you might need to turn off the safety. Look in the bottom middle of your screen, above your hands, while holding the gun. If you see a red or grey switch, that's the safety! Red means that it's UNSAFE, and can fire. Grey means that it's safe and can fire. Firing when on SAFE will tell you and simply make the gun go click.
