# Overview

Combat is the act of killing something before it kills you. There are many ways of going about combat, including different types of weapons, grab combat, and even unarmed combat. This guide will tell you the basics and a few more things about each type of combat to prepare you for a server that's a little more combat-oriented than most. 

# Melee Combat
Melee combat is the art of smacking someone with something while standing near them. Most items in the game have a FORCE variable which determines the amount of damage they will deal when hitting someone. The larger or more weapon-shaped something is, the more damage it deals. You can increase the damage of the hit by either wielding the item (hold the item in your hand and press SHIFT + X in Hotkey mode or this button in the bottom right of your screen)

## Unarmed Combat
Unarmed combat usually has no weapons. There are some gloves, however, that can increase your melee damage. Look in the crafting menu for spiked knuckles or ask your local engineer or craftsperson to make some for you! Unarmed combat also scales in damage with your [Robustness](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills) stat.

### Martial Arts
Grab-fu is the act of tossing someone around until they die, unlike regular unarmed combat where you beat the tar out of someone. There are many things you can do with a grab, but first you have to grab them. To grab someone, enter <span style= "color:yellow"> GRAB intent </span> and click your target. You will see a little hand pop up in the hand you used to click the person or animal once you've successfully grabbed them. Once you've managed to grab, either press Z or click the grab in your active hand. This upgrades the grab from a <span style = "color:yellow"> passive (yellow) </span> to <span style = "color:teal">aggressive (blue)</span>. Do this again to upgrade from an <span style = "color:teal">aggressive (blue)</span> grab to a <span style = "color:red">neck (red)</span> grab.

## Melee Weaponry

Melee weaponry augments your damage. It scales with your [Robustness](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills) stat. When partaking in armed melee combat, you have a few choices. Standard melee weapons, i.e. not halberds, spears, and other poking weapons, can only hit mobs adjacent to you. Holding them in one hand allows you to free up the other hand for other items, like shields or health items, but gives you lower damage than if you weilded it in both hands. 

When on <span style = "color:red"> Harm intent </span>, most wielded melee weapons will sweep, allowing you to hit all mobs on the tile you click on plus the two tiles adjacent to you. This will hit ALL mobs on tiles swept, so watch out for friendly fire!

### Halberd and Spears
Halberds and spears, plus similarly long weapons, have range. When wielded, they can strike not just the tile that's adjacent to you but tiles two steps away from you as well! Use this to keep mobs or players at bay if they have a strong melee attack.

## Safety and Firemode

Before you can fire a weapon, you might need to turn off the safety. Look in the bottom middle of your screen, above your hands, while holding the gun. If you see a red or grey switch, that's the safety! Red means that it's UNSAFE, and can fire. Grey means that it's safe and can fire.
