# Personal Objectives and Tasks
Personal Objectives and Tasks are mini-objectives that can reward you with a variety of bonuses depending on the task you receive. Each has different mechanical bonuses and requirements.

# Personal Objectives
Personal Objectives are gained by leveling up. There are a lot of personal objectives, some of which are locked to departments and some of which are not. 

> THIS SECTION IS A WORK IN PROGRESS.
{.is-danger}


# Tasks

Tasks are unlocked either at the start of the game or by unlocking them throughout the round. When you have a task unlocked, certain actions give you experience towards that task. Once you've earned enough, you gain a level and the associated reward. 

## Pre-Unlocked Tasks:
The following tasks start unlocked when the round begins:
### Return to Sender:
- Each time you die, take Reviver, or are defibbed you'll gain experience in this task. 
- For each level, you gain +2 [VIV](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills). 
- You also gain level * 15 max nutrition, meaning you can eat more.
- This means at level 1 you have 415 max, then at level 2 445 (415 + (2 * 15)), then at level 3 490 (445 + (3*15)), and so on. 
### Rebound Case
- Each time you die, you gain experience in this task. This tracks differently to Rebound Case.
- For each level, you lose 5 [COG](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills), but gain 5 health and max health. 
- Non-silicon mobs also receive +10 max blood volume for each level of this task.
### When it Rains...
- Each time you gani a negative perk, you gain experience.
- Negative perks include revival sickness and perks granted from oddities.
- Each time you gain a level, you gain the [perk](/guides/starter-guides/Perks) Forceful Rejection.
### Parkour
- Each time you dive, you gain experience equal to the distance in tiles that you dove.
- For each level, you need less hunger to dive.
### Face Planting Trips
- Each time you trip on underplating, you gain experience.
- For each level, your chance to trip on underplating while running is reduced.
### Slab Clearer
- Each time you use the Slab Console, you gain experience. The Slab Console is the console that pulls up a large amount of ores and walls to mine. 
- For each level, you gain 1 additional ore when mining or digging.
 
## Unlockable Tasks:
The following tasks are able to be unlocked during the round. To unlock them, you perform their actions until you gain 1 level in the task. After that, the second and further levels will provide their reward.
### Self Motivation
- Unlocked by: Completing a Personal Objective.
- Each time you complete a personal objective, you gain a level in this task.
- For each level, you gain 3 [insight](/guides/starter-guides/Sanity-and-Insight) + 1 insight per level you have. You also gain the same amount of resting insight. 
### Clay Thumb
- Unlocked by: Caring for plants.
- Each time you care for plants, be it watering, weeding, or similar (even potted plants that need water), you gain experience towards your next level. 
- For each level, you gain 2 [BIO](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills).
### Vendor Shopper
- Unlocked by: Purchasing from vendors.
- Each time you purchase from a vendor, you gain 1% of the purchase price as experience.
- For each level, you gain 2 [COG](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills).
### Body Builder
- Unlocked by using gym machines.
- Each time you use a gym machine, you gain experience. You gain experience at 5x the normal rate if you use them while you have an [insight point](/guides/starter-guides/Sanity-and-Insight) stored (i.e. if you've leveled up but not chosen to spend the point yet.
- For each level you gain Level + 2 [VIV](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills), level*5 max nutrition, and 10 blood volume.
	- For example, at level 1 you gain 3 VIV (1 + 2), 5 max nutrition (5 * 1), and 10 blood volume. Total nutrition - 105 (assuming base of 100) and total blood volume - 110 (assuming base 100.) Gaining level 2 would grant 4 VIV (2 + 2), 10 max nutrition (5 * 2), and 10 blood volume. Total nutrition - 115 and total blood volume 120.
### Dr. Floor
- Unlocked by eating enough floor pills. 
- Each time you eat a Floor Pill, you gain experience. Floor Pills are randomspawn Floor Pills, usually found in maintenance or loot piles.
- For each level, you gain level + 2 [VIV](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills).
### Proper Sealer
- Unlocked by sealing burrows.
- Each time you dig out and seal a burrow, you gain experience. 
- For each level, you gain level + 1 [MEC](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills) and TGH.
### Tool Consumer
- Unlocked by brekaing a tool. 
- Each time you break a tool, you gain experience.
- For each level, you gain Level + 1 [MEC](/guides/starter-guides/Stats-and-Skills). Tool-related difficulty levels are also reduced by your number of levels in this task. 
### Smoking Area
- Unlocked by smoking in proper smoking areas, including the bar.
- While smoking in a smoking area, you gain experience.
- For each level, you gain maximum sanity equal to your level. For example, starting with 100 SAN you then gain 2 for a total of 102 (level one is skipped because you unlock it at level 1), then at level 3 you gain 3 for a total of 105.
### Learned Style
- Unlocked by unsheathing weapons. You need to unsheath a *lot* to do this, as each rank requires 10x the number of unsheathes as the one before it.
- Each time you unsheath a weapon, you gain 1 experience.
- For each level, your melee weapons gain +1 range for the first attack after drawn from a sheath.
